# Integration logo sources

These are service brand marks, bundled locally to avoid runtime requests to logo CDNs.

- GitHub, Notion, MCP: `@lobehub/icons-static-svg` via jsDelivr (github.svg, notion.svg, mcp.svg).
- Slack: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d5/Slack_icon_2019.svg
- Gmail: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/7e/Gmail_icon_%282020%29.svg
- WhatsApp: https://cdn.simpleicons.org/whatsapp
- Jira: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@11/icons/jira.svg (brand blue applied).

Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners.
